### Full description

Paste as plain paragraphs, without bullets. The first 500 characters show before "Read more", so the first paragraph carries the pitch.

```
Turn your next product release into an event. Drop Engine lets you schedule a launch for any Wix Stores products, show shoppers a live countdown, and keep checkout closed until the exact moment your drop opens. Customers can see the products and add them to the cart early, but they can only buy once the launch window starts.

Set a start and end time in the time zone you announce the launch in. Launch times are checked against the server's clock, so changing the time on a shopper's device won't open a drop early. When the window closes, you decide what happens next: restore normal purchasing, or keep the products unavailable until you're ready.

The countdown appears on your product page automatically and shows each product's launch status. You can also place the countdown element anywhere on your site and match it to your design with your own fonts and colors. It supports right-to-left languages and adapts to mobile screens.

Build anticipation before launch day with a built-in waitlist. Shoppers sign up from the product page with clear consent, duplicate signups are filtered out, and you can review every entry from your dashboard.

From the dashboard you can save drafts, publish, edit, duplicate, cancel, and archive drops. Each drop moves from Scheduled to Live to Ended. If plans change, cancel a drop and checkout reopens right away. A step-by-step guide and FAQ are included.

Drop Engine works with Wix Stores.
```

## Get Found (App Profile > App Market > Get Found)

### Category

```
Sell Online
```

### Search keywords (maximum 5)

```
countdown timer
product drop
product launch
coming soon
waitlist
```

Why these five:

- **countdown timer** is the phrase merchants type most often for this kind of app. "countdown" alone is too broad.
- **product drop** matches the streetwear and limited-release audience, and the app name.
- **product launch** covers merchants who don't use the word "drop".
- **coming soon** catches merchants looking to tease products before release, which is what the countdown and blocked checkout do.
- **waitlist** is a shipped feature with clear intent.

Do not use "pre-order". The app blocks checkout until launch, so it does not take orders early. Merchants who install it for pre-orders will uninstall and may leave a bad review. Avoid "sale" and "flash sale" for the same reason: the app has no discount features.

Backups, if a keyword underperforms: `limited release`, `launch countdown`, `scheduled release`, `sneaker drop`.
